Our Story

A Jaipur Factory Outlet for Ethnic Fashion

Jaipur Vibes is a factory outlet, direct manufacturer and wholesaler focused on women’s ethnic fashion. Our collections span designer kurtis, palazzos, dupattas and fabrics, and we welcome wholesale and bulk enquiries from businesses as well as visits from customers to our outlet.
